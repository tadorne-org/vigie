The **Tadorne** iPhone app displays the readings that Vigie nodes advertise
over Bluetooth Low Energy.

- **No data is collected.** The app has no account, no analytics, no
  advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK.
- **Nothing leaves the phone.** The app makes no network request. It only
  listens to Bluetooth advertisements; it never connects to a device.
- **What is stored, and where.** Readings received while the app is open,
  the names given to nodes, and their Bluetooth addresses are kept in the
  app's own storage on the phone. BTHome encryption keys entered by the user
  are kept in the iOS keychain. Deleting a node, or the app, erases them.
- **Bluetooth permission** is used only to receive Vigie advertisements.
- **Export.** A CSV export is created only when the user asks for it, and
  goes only where the user sends it.

The enclosure's sole is printed. It is a single flexible piece, in **TPU 95A**,
that lines the bottom and does two jobs: it **cradles the shunt**, whose lips
close over the blocks, and it **carries the board** on four posts.

<figure>
  <img src="/img/v1-sole-print.webp" alt="Orange TPU sole mid-print on the bed of an Ender printer.">
</figure>

<figure>
  <img src="/img/v1-sole.webp" alt="Finished TPU 95A sole held in hand: flat underside, shunt cradle along one edge.">
</figure>

A solid buffer, bonded under the lid, presses on the cradle with 1.5 mm of
preload: the shunt is clamped between the bottom and the lid, with no play.
Slab in 25% gyroid, cradle printed solid, 0.15 mm layers, no supports.

<figure>
  <img src="/img/v1-enclosure.webp" alt="The node mounted in its enclosure: shunt on the left, Vigie 1.0 board at centre above its TPU sole.">
</figure>

The node is mounted in its enclosure. Wiring and the measurements on a battery
are left to do.

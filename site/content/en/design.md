Vigie is not drawn by hand. Its schematic, footprint placement and routing are produced by scripts kept with the design, so the same command rebuilds the same files.

- The schematic comes from a single description of the components and the nets.
- Board outline, mounting holes, footprint placement and the internal ground and 3.3 V planes are scripted.
- Signal routing is delegated to a router on the two outer layers only; the inner layers stay solid planes.
- Every build ends with a netlist check pin by pin, an electrical rule check and a design rule check.

{{< callout title="Board" >}}
86 × 54 mm, four layers, sixty-nine footprints, fifty-four nets. Measurement across an external 100 A shunt, four-wire.
{{< /callout >}}
